# Coffee Research Checklist

Detailed procedures for researching coffee beans.

---

## Web Search Strategy

### Primary Sources (in order of reliability)
1. **Roaster's website** — Most accurate for their own coffee
2. **Specialty coffee databases** — Trade Coffee, Bean Box listings
3. **Coffee review sites** — detailed tasting notes and roast analysis

### Search Queries
- `"[roaster name]" "[coffee name]"` — exact match
- `"[roaster name]" [origin] [process]` — if name unclear
- `[origin] [variety] espresso` — for general origin guidance

---

## Inferring Roast Level

When roast level isn't stated, infer from tasting notes:

### Light Roast Indicators
- Fruit-forward: blueberry, strawberry, citrus, stone fruit
- Floral: jasmine, lavender, bergamot
- Tea-like descriptors
- "Bright," "vibrant," "complex acidity"
- Wine-like or fermented notes (especially naturals)

### Medium Roast Indicators
- Balanced fruit + chocolate
- Nutty: almond, hazelnut
- Caramel, brown sugar
- "Balanced," "smooth," "approachable"
- Some origin character with sweetness

### Dark Roast Indicators
- Chocolate dominant: dark chocolate, cocoa
- Roasty: toasted, smoky
- Low acidity descriptors
- "Bold," "full-bodied," "rich"
- Lacking origin-specific fruit notes

## Origin Extraction Patterns

### Ethiopian
- **Character:** Fruit-forward, floral, complex
- **Processing:** Often natural (fruit bomb) or washed (floral, tea-like)
- **Approach:** Higher temps (94-96°C), bloom profiles for naturals
- **Ratio:** 1:2.5 for fruit development

### Colombian
- **Character:** Balanced, sweet, mild acidity
- **Processing:** Mostly washed
- **Approach:** Classic 9-bar works well, 92-94°C
- **Ratio:** 1:2 standard

### Brazilian
- **Character:** Nutty, chocolate, low acidity
- **Processing:** Natural or pulped natural common
- **Approach:** Lower temps (90-93°C), works in milk
- **Ratio:** 1:2 or shorter for intensity

### Kenyan
- **Character:** Bright, berry, wine-like acidity
- **Processing:** Washed (double fermentation)
- **Approach:** High temps (94-96°C) to extract sweetness
- **Ratio:** 1:2.5 to 1:3 for clarity

### Indonesian (Sumatra, Java)
- **Character:** Earthy, herbal, full body
- **Processing:** Wet-hulled (Giling Basah)
- **Approach:** Lower temps (90-92°C), shorter ratios
- **Ratio:** 1:1.5 to 1:2 for body

### Central American (Guatemala, Costa Rica, Honduras)
- **Character:** Balanced, chocolate, mild fruit
- **Processing:** Mostly washed
- **Approach:** Classic profiles, 92-94°C
- **Ratio:** 1:2 standard

## Processing Method Patterns

### Washed (Wet Process)
- **Character:** Clean, bright, origin-forward
- **Extraction:** Straightforward, classic profiles work well
- **Pre-infusion:** Standard 4-6 seconds sufficient
- **Notes:** Most forgiving processing method

### Natural (Dry Process)
- **Character:** Fruity, wine-like, fermented notes
- **Extraction:** Benefits from bloom phase to control intensity
- **Pre-infusion:** Extended (6-10 seconds) + bloom
- **Notes:** Can be overwhelming without proper extraction control

### Honey (Pulped Natural)
- **Character:** Sweetness between washed and natural
- **Extraction:** Moderate pre-infusion (5-7 seconds)
- **Notes:** Yellow honey = cleaner, black honey = more fruit

### Anaerobic
- **Character:** Intense, funky, unique fermentation flavors
- **Extraction:** Careful temperature control, often lower
- **Pre-infusion:** Extended to manage intensity
- **Notes:** Can overwhelm if over-extracted

## Freshness Adjustment

### Days Off Roast Impact

| Days Off Roast | CO2 Level | Grind Adjustment |
|----------------|-----------|------------------|
| 3-7 days | High | 1-2 micro steps finer |
| 8-14 days | Medium | Baseline (grind map reference) |
| 15-21 days | Low | 1-2 micro steps coarser |
| 22+ days | Depleted | Consider fresher beans |

### Calculation Example

If grind-map.md shows a similar coffee at `9D` at 14 days off roast:
- New bag at 7 days → suggest `9B` or `9C` (finer)
- New bag at 21 days → suggest `9F` or `9G` (coarser)

## Photo Extraction Guide

When reading a coffee bag photo, look for:

1. **Roaster name** — Usually prominent branding
2. **Coffee name** — Often a farm, region, or creative name
3. **Origin** — Country, region, farm name
4. **Process** — May say "washed," "natural," "honey," etc.
5. **Roast date** — "Roasted on [date]" — critical for freshness
6. **Tasting notes** — Flavor descriptors
7. **Altitude** — Sometimes listed (higher = denser = finer grind)
8. **Variety** — Bourbon, Typica, Gesha, SL28, etc.

If any info is missing from the bag, search for it online.
