# Research Plan D: Dialing In & Profile Tuning

The iterative improvement loop: taste → diagnose → adjust. These three topics form the feedback cycle that makes each shot better than the last.

## Research Topics

### D1. Dialing-In Methodology — MODERATE

**Current:** "Quick Start" in ESPRESSO_BREWING_BASICS, the 5g rule, adjustment tables.

**Gaps:** Systematic approach (start fine vs start coarse — experts disagree), salami shot technique, realistic shot count expectations (3-5 typical), when to abandon an approach, separating grind/temp/profile problems, "sour AND bitter = channeling" diagnostic (Scott Rao), diminishing returns concept, handling multiple coffees in rotation.

**Sources:** Scott Rao, James Hoffmann "Dialing In" series, Lance Hedrick.

### D2. Profile Phase Modification (Taste-Driven) — MODERATE

**Current:** Profile troubleshooting covers grind/temp/dose. Profile library has "By Problem" selection.

**Gaps:** Modifying the *profile itself* based on taste — bloom duration tuning, ramp speed effects (faster → body, slower → clarity), decline aggressiveness, pre-infusion flow rate tuning, phase duration indicators (too long vs too short), when to add/remove decline, when bloom helps vs unnecessary, temperature profiling.

**Sources:** Scott Rao (bloom research), Decent Espresso community, Gaggimate Discord.

### D3. Sensory Development — MODERATE

**Current:** ESPRESSO_TASTING_GUIDE covers sour vs bitter and basic evaluation.

**Gaps:** SCA Flavor Wheel reference, palate development exercises, cupping espresso (diluting to evaluate), temperature-dependent flavor changes, salami shot as diagnostic, aroma evaluation, common off-flavor identification (woody = old, rubbery = robusta, medicinal = defect).

**Sources:** SCA Flavor Wheel, James Hoffmann, Barista Hustle sensory module, Counter Culture.
