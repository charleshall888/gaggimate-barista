# Research Plan A: Extraction Science Fundamentals

The physics and chemistry of what happens when water meets coffee. These three topics are deeply interconnected — fines affect channeling, channeling affects extraction yield, extraction yield is measured by TDS.

## Research Topics

### A1. Extraction Science (TDS & Extraction Yield) — SIGNIFICANT

**Current:** Conceptual coverage of extraction stages. Extraction yield numbers mentioned but never explained.

**Gaps:** What TDS measures, how to calculate extraction yield, target EY ranges by style, the Brewing Control Chart, refractometer usage, concentration vs extraction distinction, which variables move EY most.

**Sources:** Scott Rao, Barista Hustle "Extraction" module, Jonathan Gagné (coffeeadastra.com), VST/DiFluid documentation.

### A2. Grind Science — SIGNIFICANT

**Current:** Sette 270 mechanics/calibration/maintenance documented well. No general grind theory.

**Gaps:** Bimodal vs unimodal particle distribution, conical vs flat burr character differences, how the Sette 270's conical burrs shape extraction, fines role in over-extraction and channeling, grind retention effects, why different grinders need different profiles.

**Sources:** Jonathan Gagné "The Physics of Filter Coffee", James Hoffmann grinder comparisons, Lance Hedrick, Socratic Coffee.

### A3. Channeling Physics — SIGNIFICANT

**Current:** Mentioned throughout with basic fixes (WDT, pre-infusion, paper filter). Telemetry diagnostics documented.

**Gaps:** Physics of why channeling occurs (positive feedback loop), fines migration mechanism, visual diagnosis with bottomless portafilter, variable hierarchy (distribution > tamping > pressure), paper filter and puck screen roles, why pre-infusion prevents channeling.

**Sources:** James Hoffmann, Jonathan Gagné, Scott Rao, Decent Espresso community.
