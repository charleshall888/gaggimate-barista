## What to Expect (When Fresh)

Pink Bourbon is genetically an Ethiopian landrace variety — not a true Bourbon hybrid, despite the name. Discovered in San Adolfo, Huila around 2014. This explains its unusually fruit-forward, floral character for a Colombian coffee.

- **Origin character:** Huila's volcanic soil and altitude produce structured, clean coffees with pronounced acidity and sweetness. Natural processing on top of this base adds a syrupy, fruit-forward layer.
- **Variety (Pink Bourbon):** Ethiopian landrace genetics mean sweet, floral, tropical fruit rather than the typical Bourbon caramel/nut profile. High sugar content in the cherry makes it particularly well-suited to natural processing.
- **Processing (Natural):** Amplifies fruit-forward character — expect strawberry, raspberry, tropical fruit, plum, and a wine-like depth. Huila naturals extract slightly more readily than washed due to fermentation breaking down some cell wall structure.
- **Density/Altitude:** 1,500 masl is mid-range for Huila (lower end of ideal Pink Bourbon range). Moderately dense bean — good structure but not as austere as 1,800+ masl lots.

Libertario is a cousin brand to La Palma y el Tucan, one of Colombia's most respected specialty producers and exporters. The SCA 88.5 score and careful Pink Bourbon sourcing reflects that lineage.
