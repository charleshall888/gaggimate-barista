## What to Expect

Extended 120-hour anaerobic fermentation on a high-altitude Ecuador Typica — expect more intensity and fruit complexity than the Natural from the same farm, but with Typica's characteristic clarity keeping it precise rather than wild. The raspberry and stone fruit should be vivid; the cocoa note grounds it with some roast development.

- **Origin character:** Ecuador Loja produces transparent, structured naturals. Even with 120hr anaerobic, expect precision over jammy chaos — this isn't an Ethiopian natural screaming fermented fruit.
- **Variety (Typica):** Known for clean flavor expression and floral aromatics. A good carrier for the fermented fruit notes without muddying them.
- **Processing (Anaerobic 120hr):** Extended fermentation significantly increases solubility of aromatic compounds. Grind slightly finer than the farm's Natural baseline but coarser than equivalently light Ethiopian anaerobics. Start between those two anchors.
- **Density/Altitude:** 2000 MASL = dense bean. Holds up well to bloom profiling; supports moderate pressure (7.5 bar peak) cleanly.

### First Shot Diagnostics

| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Sour or fast (< 24s) | Go finer: try 13D |
| Chokes or very slow (> 35s) | First: recheck WDT and tamp evenness — Sette 270 fines migration is the likely cause. If puck prep is solid, go coarser to 13F |
| Fruit notes flat, not bitter | Raise temp to 95°C before touching grind |
| Raspberry / stone fruit clear and sweet | Locked in |
