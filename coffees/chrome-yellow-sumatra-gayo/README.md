## What to Expect

Sumatran naturals are a rarity — the region almost exclusively uses wet-hulling (Giling Basah), which produces earthy, heavy character. Chrome Yellow's natural processing strips that away and lets the fruit come forward, resulting in a coffee that behaves more like an African natural than a typical Sumatra.

- **Origin character:** Central Aceh is Sumatra's premium Arabica belt — volcanic soil, humid climate. Expect heavier body and earthy sweetness as a baseline, with natural processing lifting it significantly toward fruit and florals.
- **Varieties (Catimor/Tim Tim/Ateng Super):** Hardy hybrids bred for disease resistance. They tend toward fuller body, lower inherent acidity, and heavier mouthfeel than Ethiopian heirlooms — this is where "creamy" comes from.
- **Processing (Natural):** Dry-processed on raised beds amplifies fruit sweetness and florals in ways wet-hulling never could. The strawberry and floral notes are entirely a product of this processing choice.
- **Altitude (1,400–1,600 masl):** Moderate density compared to Ethiopian high-altitude coffees. Slightly easier to extract — contributes to the creamy, approachable body rather than the piercing brightness of Ethiopian naturals.
