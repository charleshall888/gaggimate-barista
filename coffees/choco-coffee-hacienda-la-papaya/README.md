**Variety note:** The bag lists "Typica" but markets this as "Pacamara Reserve." Hacienda La Papaya (Juan Peña, Saraguro) is known for Typica Mejorado. The "Pacamara" likely refers to a specific reserve plot, not the Pacamara hybrid variety. If the first shot profile skews stone fruit / chocolate / long finish, it may actually be Pacamara — treat the first shot as the real test.

## What to Expect

High-altitude Ecuador natural from a farm that prioritizes genetics and controlled fermentation. Expect delicacy and clarity over the intense fermented fruit of Ethiopian anaerobics — the melon and hibiscus notes should be clean and precise rather than jammy. The marmalade and buttered toast notes suggest a touch of Maillard development, which will anchor the lighter aromatics nicely.

- **Origin character:** Ecuador Loja produces transparent, elegant naturals with good acidity structure. Less wild than Ethiopian naturals — more precision than power.
- **Variety (Typica Mejorado):** Foundational variety known for clean flavor, floral aromatics, and balanced sweetness. Good clarity even with natural processing.
- **Processing (Natural):** Adds fruit sweetness and body without anaerobic fermentation intensity. Bloom profiling ensures even saturation and lets the melon/hibiscus notes emerge without muddiness.
- **Density/Altitude:** 2000 MASL = dense bean. Holds up well to bloom profiling; extract cleanly at moderate pressure (7-8 bar).

### First Shot Diagnostics

| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Sour or fast | Go 1-2 micro steps finer |
| Bitter or slow / choke | Go 1-2 micro steps coarser (try 14A) |
| Melon/hibiscus absent, toast/marmalade dominant | Shorter ratio or coarser grind first — this is over-extraction, not low temp |
| Flat, no character | Raise temp to 95°C |
| Stone fruit, chocolate, long finish instead of melon/citrus | You may have Pacamara — dial for longer ratio, slightly coarser |
