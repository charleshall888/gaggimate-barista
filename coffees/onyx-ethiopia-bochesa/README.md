## What to Expect

A fruit-forward anaerobic natural from one of Ethiopia's highest-altitude stations. The 36-hour limited oxygen fermentation amplifies the berry and tropical notes well beyond a standard natural, while the Arbegona terroir at 2,248 masl contributes density and complexity. Expect intense blueberry and cranberry sweetness with a mead-like honeyed fermentation character.

- **Origin character:** Arbegona (Sidamo region) produces clean, fruit-forward naturals with bright acidity and lighter body — the high altitude adds a crystalline sweetness that separates these from lower-grown Ethiopian naturals.
- **Processing (Anaerobic Natural):** The sealed 36-hour fermentation concentrates fruit esters beyond a standard sun-dried natural. This is why we're running reduced pressure (7.5 bar) with a bloom — it controls the fermentation intensity and prevents those funky notes from tipping into harshness.
- **Density/Altitude:** At 2,248 masl this is very high density. The beans need more extraction energy — hence 95°C and a bloom phase to ensure thorough, even saturation before ramping pressure.
