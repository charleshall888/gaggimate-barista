## What to Expect

A fruit-forward natural Ethiopian with anaerobic fermentation amplifying the already intense berry and floral notes. The 74110 variety at 2100 masl produces a dense, complex bean that rewards patient extraction — expect a juicy, almost candy-like sweetness when dialed in well.

- **Origin character:** Ethiopian Yirgacheffe naturals are the archetype of fruit-bomb espresso — big blueberry and strawberry, floral aromatics, and a lighter, tea-like body compared to washed lots from the same region.
- **Variety (74110):** An Ethiopian landrace selection with wide genetic diversity. Tends toward bright, complex cups but can be unpredictable — expect the profile to need fine-tuning as flavors shift with degassing.
- **Processing (Natural + Anaerobic):** Double fermentation intensity — the anaerobic phase (7-10 days in sealed tanks) amplifies fruit esters beyond a standard natural. This is why we chose a bloom profile at reduced pressure (7.5 bar): it gives the dense puck time to open up without over-extracting the fermentation-driven flavors into harshness.
- **Density/Altitude:** At 2100 masl this is a high-density bean. It can handle higher temperatures (94°C) and benefits from thorough pre-infusion to ensure even water penetration through the hard cell structure.
