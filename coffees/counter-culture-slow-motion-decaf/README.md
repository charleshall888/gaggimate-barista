## What to Expect

A comforting, chocolate-forward decaf designed for milk drinks. Swiss Water Process preserves more of the green coffee's original character than solvent-based methods, which means more sweetness and body, less papery aftertaste. The medium roast keeps it approachable with familiar caramel and cocoa notes — an easy-going base for a cappuccino or cortado.

- **Decaf structure:** SWP decaffeination increases bean porosity, which means faster flow at the same grind setting as regular coffee. Expect to grind significantly coarser (2–3 macro steps on the Sette 270) and use lower pressure than you would for a comparable regular medium roast.
- **Processing (SWP):** Gentler on flavor than MC or EA processes; molasses/chocolate notes are authentic to the coffee, not artifacts of processing. Lower pressure suits the porous puck.
- **Freshness:** Decaf stales faster than regular coffee — target this within 7–14 days of roast for peak flavor.
